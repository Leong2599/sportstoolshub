## Understanding Vertical Jump Metrics

Vertical jump analysis turns a single reach measurement into the power, force, and percentile figures that tell you what your jump actually says about your athleticism.

### Jump Height Standards

- **High School:** 18-24 inches average
- **College:** 24-28 inches average
- **Professional:** 28-36+ inches
- **Elite:** 40+ inches

### Power Output

Power = Force x Velocity. Your vertical jump power indicates explosive strength potential and correlates with:

- Sprinting speed
- Quickness on court
- Rebounding ability

### Position Requirements

- **Point Guard:** Focus on quickness
- **Shooting Guard:** Balance height and agility
- **Small Forward:** All-around athleticism
- **Power Forward:** Strength and elevation
- **Center:** Maximum height advantage

### Training Recommendations

- **Plyometrics:** Box jumps, depth jumps
- **Strength:** Squats, deadlifts
- **Olympic lifts:** Power cleans, snatches
- **Core stability:** Planks, medicine balls
