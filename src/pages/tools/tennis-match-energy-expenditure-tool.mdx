## Understanding Tennis Match Energy

Tennis is a stop-start sport with long rallies and repeated explosive accelerations, so your energy expenditure depends as much on your playing style, court surface, and the weather as it does on how long you were on court.

### Calorie Burn Rates

- **Casual Play:** 6-8 cal/min
- **Club Match:** 8-10 cal/min
- **Tournament Match:** 10-14 cal/min
- **Professional:** 14-18+ cal/min

### Surface Energy Impact

- **Hard Court:** Baseline energy cost
- **Clay Court:** +15% energy use
- **Grass Court:** +10% energy use
- **Carpet Court:** -5% energy use

### Hydration Needs

- Normal conditions: 500-750ml/hour
- Hot/humid: 750-1000ml/hour
- High intensity: +25% fluid needs
- Monitor urine color as guide

### Recovery Requirements

- **Light match (under 60min):** 24 hours
- **Medium match (60-90min):** 48 hours
- **Long match (over 90min):** 72+ hours
- Focus on carbs and protein
