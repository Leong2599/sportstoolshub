## Understanding Match Load

A soccer match places a very large and very specific load on the body. Match load scoring combines the distance you covered, how much of it was at high intensity, how often you sprinted, how long you were on the pitch, and how well you performed.

### Distance Standards by Position

- **Goalkeeper:** 3-5 km average
- **Center Back:** 9-11 km average
- **Fullback:** 10-12 km average
- **Central Midfielder:** 11-13 km average
- **Winger:** 10-11 km average
- **Forward:** 8-10 km average

### High-Intensity Running

High-intensity distance includes running above 5.5 m/s (19.8 km/h):

- Professional average: 600-800 meters
- Elite players: 900+ meters
- Key indicator of match fitness

### Recovery Guidelines

- **Low Load (under 8):** 24-48 hours recovery
- **Moderate Load (8-12):** 48-72 hours recovery
- **High Load (over 12):** 72+ hours recovery
- Consider nutrition and sleep quality

### Environmental Factors

- **Heat (over 25C):** +20% load increase
- **Cold (under 5C):** +10% load increase
- **Humidity:** Affects dehydration risk
- **Altitude:** Adjust intensity expectations
