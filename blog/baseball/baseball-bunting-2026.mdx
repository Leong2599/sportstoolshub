## Why bunting matters in Baseball

Bunting is a control skill that can move runners, score runs, and surprise a defence. Good bunters start from a stable base, get the bat out early, and use their hands to direct the ball. A clear focus makes feedback useful, because you know exactly which variable you are trying to change.

Baseball places specific demands on the body and the decision-maker: explosive rotational swings, repeated accelerations and decelerations, overhand throwing, and long periods of standing that precede sudden sprint efforts. This skill is where those demands meet technique, so improving it makes every surrounding skill easier to express.

## Technique Essentials

These principles separate functional technique from busy practice:

- Square around early enough to see the pitch, but keep the bat still until the ball arrives.
- Hold the bat with the bottom hand near the knob and the top hand supporting the barrel.
- Keep the barrel above the ball and angle it toward the desired side of the infield.
- Use the legs and a soft top hand to deaden the ball rather than absorbing it with stiff arms.
- Run out of the box immediately after contact, even on a sacrifice.

## Drills to Build the Skill

- Bunt-to-line — bunt ten balls along each foul line from a batting tee.
- Soft-hands catch — a partner tosses balls while you practise giving with the top hand.
- Sacrifice situational — runner on first, bunt to the first-base side and sprint.
- Drag-bunt walk-through — rehearse the pivot and push while moving out of the box.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Squaring too late and getting hit or missing the pitch.
- Pushing the barrel at the ball instead of letting it meet the bat.
- Bunting a strike up in the zone when the goal is to put it on the ground.
- Copying an advanced athlete's routine without adapting it to your current capacity and schedule.
- Treating one good session as proof of mastery instead of testing the skill again under different conditions.

## Terminology

- **OPS:** On-base percentage plus slugging percentage, a compact measure of overall hitting value.
- **Cut-off:** An infielder positioned to relay a throw from the outfield to a base.
- **Double play:** Two outs recorded on a single continuous play.
- **Lead-off:** The initial distance a runner takes from a base before the pitch is delivered.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Should I bunt with two strikes?**  
Usually no. A foul bunt with two strikes is a strikeout, so most hitters swing away unless the situation demands otherwise.

**What is the best bunt direction?**  
Away from the pitcher and toward the corner you want to force the defence to cover. Placement matters more than a perfect technique.

**How do I fit this into a busy week?**  
Two or three focused sessions of 30-45 minutes beat one long, unfocused session. Keep the quality high, reduce volume when time is short, and protect sleep.

## Practical Coaching Cues

Keep these cues short enough to use in the middle of a session:

- **Prepare early:** start the movement before the pressure arrives, so bunting has room to work.
- **Stay relaxed:** grip, shoulders, and face should stay loose until the moment force is required.
- **Finish balanced:** if you cannot hold the landing or follow-through for two seconds, reduce the effort.
- **Use one cue:** pick a single word or image and repeat it for the whole set.
- **Stop while quality is high:** end the drill one or two repetitions before form starts to decay.
