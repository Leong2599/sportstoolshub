## Understanding Badminton Rally Intensity

Badminton intensity is a combination of how often you rally, how long each rally lasts, how hard you strike, and how varied your shot selection is. Tracking all four gives a much better read than rally count alone.

### Rally Duration Standards

- **Beginner:** 4-6 shots per rally
- **Intermediate:** 6-10 shots per rally
- **Advanced:** 8-14 shots per rally
- **Professional:** 12-20+ shots per rally

### Shot Pattern Analysis

- **Smash Ratio:** 15-25% of total shots
- **Net Play:** 30-40% in advanced play
- **Clears:** 25-35% defensive shots
- **Drops:** 10-15% tactical shots

### Intensity Factors

- Rally length (longer = higher intensity)
- Shot speed and aggression
- Court coverage requirements
- Recovery time between rallies

### Training Implications

- **High intensity:** Focus on interval training
- **Long rallies:** Build stamina
- **Fast exchanges:** Improve reaction time
- **Shot variety:** Practice all shot types
