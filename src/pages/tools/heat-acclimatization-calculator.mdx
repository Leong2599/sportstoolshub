## Understanding Heat Acclimatization

Heat acclimatization is the process that lets your body cope with heat. Until you have it, hot weather costs you pace and raises your health risk.

### Heat Stress Levels

- **Under 18C:** No heat adjustment needed
- **18-22C:** 2-5% slower pace
- **22-26C:** 5-10% slower pace
- **26-30C:** 10-15% slower pace
- **Over 30C:** 15-20%+ slower pace

### Hydration Guidelines

- **Mild (under 20C):** 500-750ml/hour
- **Moderate (20-25C):** 750-1000ml/hour
- **Hot (25-30C):** 1000-1500ml/hour
- **Very Hot (over 30C):** 1500ml+/hour
- Add 200-300ml per 10C above 20C

### Acclimatization Timeline

- **Day 1-3:** Initial adaptation (60% benefit)
- **Day 4-7:** Significant improvement (80%)
- **Day 8-14:** Near full adaptation (95%)
- **Day 14+:** Complete acclimatization
- Benefits last 2-3 weeks without heat exposure

### Risk Factors

- High humidity (over 70%) reduces cooling
- No wind increases heat stress
- Direct sun adds 5-8C effective temperature
- Previous heat illness increases risk
- Medications can affect heat regulation
