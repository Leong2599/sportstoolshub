## Understanding Swimming Efficiency

Stroke efficiency is the balance between how far you travel per stroke and how many strokes you take to get there. The SWOLF score combines both into a single number you can track over time.

### SWOLF Score

- **What it is:** Time (seconds) + Strokes per length
- **Lower is better:** Indicates efficiency
- **Elite swimmers:** 20-25 in freestyle
- **Intermediate:** 30-40 range typical
- **Pool dependent:** Short course = lower SWOLF

### Stroke Rate

- **Distance swimming:** 45-55 strokes/minute
- **Middle distance:** 50-65 strokes/minute
- **Sprinting:** 65-85 strokes/minute
- **Triathlon:** 40-50 strokes/minute
- **Masters:** Often 40-55 for endurance

### Distance Per Stroke

- **Elite freestyle:** 2.0-2.5m per stroke
- **Advanced:** 1.8-2.2m per stroke
- **Intermediate:** 1.5-2.0m per stroke
- **Beginner:** 1.2-1.8m per stroke
- **Stroke efficiency:** Balance distance and rate

### Efficiency Factors

- Streamlining and body position
- Catch and pull technique quality
- Kicking efficiency and rhythm
- Breathing patterns and timing
- Consistency and relaxation
