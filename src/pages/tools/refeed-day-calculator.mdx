## Understanding Refeed Days

### What is a Refeed?

A strategic increase in calories, primarily from carbohydrates, designed to boost leptin, restore glycogen, and improve metabolic rate during dieting phases.

### Metabolic Benefits

Refeeds can temporarily increase metabolic rate by 3-10%, improve hormone function, and break through weight loss plateaus after extended dieting.

### Psychological Relief

Planned refeeds provide mental breaks from dieting, reduce cravings, and improve long-term adherence to cutting phases.

### Carb Loading Effect

Refeeds replenish muscle glycogen stores, improving training performance and subsequent workouts during the cutting phase.
