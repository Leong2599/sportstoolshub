## Understanding Weight Class Optimization

Choosing a weight class is a trade-off between size, speed, and how much weight you have to cut to make the limit.

### Weight Cutting Guidelines

- **Safe weekly loss:** 1-1.5% body weight
- **Maximum water cut:** 5-7% body weight
- **Recovery time:** 24+ hours for every 2% cut
- **Medical supervision:** Required for cuts over 5%

### Competitive Factors

- Reach advantage vs weight advantage
- Power-to-weight ratio optimization
- Recovery between fights
- Long-term health considerations

### Performance Metrics

- **Strength:** Declines with excessive cutting
- **Endurance:** Affected by dehydration
- **Speed:** May increase at lower weights
- **Recovery:** Slower with larger cuts

### Strategic Considerations

- Talent density in weight classes
- Body type suitability
- Career trajectory goals
- Longevity vs immediate advantage
